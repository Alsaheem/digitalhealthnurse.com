---

One of the most common challenges I hear from nurses and healthcare professionals is this:

“I know my experience is valuable — I just don’t know how to explain it.”

That is normal. Clinical environments have their own language. Digital health and health tech roles often use a different one.

## Shift from tasks to outcomes

Instead of listing only what you did, describe the outcome you helped create.

For example:

- “Supported patients through complex treatment pathways” becomes “helped people navigate complex systems under pressure”
- “Used electronic records daily” becomes “worked confidently with clinical systems and data-driven workflows”
- “Coordinated multidisciplinary care” becomes “aligned different stakeholders around shared patient goals”

The experience is the same. The framing changes.

## Make it specific

Hiring managers respond well to concrete detail:

- Who you worked with
- What problem you were solving
- What improved as a result

You do not need to oversell. Clear, honest examples are usually more persuasive than broad claims.

## Keep the story consistent

Your CV, LinkedIn profile and interview answers should tell the same story: you are a healthcare professional who understands care delivery and wants to apply that experience in a different setting.

If you are unsure how to position your experience for a specific role, a focused mentoring conversation can help you practise that story with clarity and confidence.
